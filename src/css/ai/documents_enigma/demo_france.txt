France is a country in Western Europe. Its capital and largest city is Paris, which sits on the Seine River. Paris is known for the Eiffel Tower, which was completed in 1889 for the World's Fair, and for the Louvre, the most visited art museum in the world. France has a population of about 68 million people, and the official language is French. The country is famous for its cuisine, including baguettes, cheese, and pastries such as croissants. Other major French cities include Marseille on the Mediterranean coast, Lyon, known for its food, and Toulouse, home to a large aerospace industry. France is also a leading producer of wine, with famous regions such as Bordeaux, Burgundy, and Champagne.
